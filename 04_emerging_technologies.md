# ♾+🥽+🎮 Augmented Reality/Virtual Reality (AR/VR)

## Purpose
The main purpose of Augmented Reality (AR) is to enhance the real world by overlaying digital information, while Virtual Reality (VR) aims to create a completely immersive digital environment that users can interact with. Essentially, AR adds to what we already see, while VR transports us to a new, simulated reality. 

- **Reference**:  
  _Source: Watts, S. (2024, December 4). “Augmented vs. Virtual Reality: Comparing AR/VR.” Splunk Blogs. Retrieved from [https://www.splunk.com/en_us/blog/learn/ar-vr.html](https://www.splunk.com/en_us/blog/learn/ar-vr.html)_

## Usage
- **In Education**:  
 AR and VR enrich learning by making abstract or complex subjects tangible. For example, AR can superimpose a beating 3D human heart on a table, or VR can transport students into a virtual ancient civilization

  - **Reference**:  
    _Source: Crogman, H. T., Cano, V. D., Pacheco, E., Sonawane, R. B., & Boroon, R. (2025). “Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality, and Mixed Reality in Experiential Learning: Transforming Educational Paradigms.” Education Sciences, 15(3), 303. Retrieved from [https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7102/15/3/303](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7102/15/3/303)_

- **In IT**:  
  **Cloud-based AR/VR** uses a lightweight client architecture that shifts intensive processing tasks to remote servers. This allows for high-definition rendering and real-time interaction without requiring powerful local hardware, making immersive experiences more accessible and efficient.

  - **Reference**:  
    _Source: Graph AI (n.d.). “Cloud-based Augmented Reality (AR): Definition, Examples, and Applications.” Graph AI Engineering Glossary. Retrieved from [https://www.graphapp.ai/engineering-glossary/cloud-computing/cloud-based-augmented-reality-ar](https://www.graphapp.ai/engineering-glossary/cloud-computing/cloud-based-augmented-reality-ar)_

## Examples

- **In Education**:  
  During lockdowns, the University of Edinburgh piloted an **_“Anatomy Experience”_ AR system** for medical students. The tool improved comprehension of human anatomy and enriched the remote learning process, according to focus group feedback

  - **Reference**:  
    _Source: Korre, D., & Sherlock, A. (2023). “Augmented Reality in Higher Education: A Case Study in Medical Education.” arXiv. Retrieved from [https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16248](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16248)_

- **In IT**:  
  **Surespan** a UK firm behind high-profile projects like _SoFi Stadium_ and the _Burj Khalifa_, they uses **RealWear AR headsets** to assist remote technicians in real-time. Combined with a zero-trust network (ZTNA) model, this approach reduced travel and saved over USD 54,000, while also streamlining global collaborations

  - **Reference**:
